What each workflow is worth

Two numbers per workflow: how much time one run saves, and what an hour of the work it replaced costs. Everything on the Overview tab is those two numbers multiplied by real execution counts — so a workflow left at zero contributes nothing, rather than being estimated.

Business case asks what the manual job was and divides it by how often n8n actually ran — which is what keeps the figure tracking reality instead of an estimate made once. Per-run figures is for when you already know the number.
